## Time

Kazakhstan time is UTC+5. Korea (KST) is UTC+9, so Almaty time = KST minus 4 hours.
Always write times as Almaty time, for example: 18:00 (Алматы уақыты).

## Tone

Warm, fan to fan, respectful to the members. Short sentences. No slang that older ARMY would not understand.
Emojis: 1 to 3 per post, purple heart 💜 is the signature.
